Quality Scoring Methodology & Formula

DataGuard AI calculates overall dataset quality deterministically from applicable checks across four standardized dimensions:

Overall Quality Score = 25% Completeness + 20% Uniqueness + 30% Validity + 25% Consistency

* Statistical anomaly observations are isolated and not penalized as confirmed errors. Non-applicable checks are not penalized. Records affected by multiple checks within a dimension are deduplicated to prevent double-penalization.
